01 / THE PROJECTA city in motion

A little more
clarity, between
here and there.

Udaipur Bus is a public transportation information app designed to help people find the bus and stop details they need for getting around the city.

This independent case study focuses on the interface: how nearby stops, bus information, journey planning and saved places can come together in a clear, approachable mobile experience.

My contribution was the UI/UX design. A developer implemented the application; development and backend work were not part of my role.

07 / REFLECTIONNotes for the next journey

Everyday journeys
deserve thoughtful design.

Designing for public transportation is a reminder that clarity is not decoration. It can help an interface feel more approachable when someone is simply trying to get somewhere.

Working through this concept highlighted the importance of information hierarchy, readable details and giving bilingual audiences a clear, considered welcome.

Accessibility and everyday usability are ongoing responsibilities. A next iteration could examine these screens with commuters, test key tasks and explore the experience in varied lighting and connectivity conditions. Those are opportunities to investigate, not outcomes already measured.

A work in progress, like every good route.
